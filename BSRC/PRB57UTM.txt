      * 5 NOV 2025 PGK                                                 *
      *    THIS JOB UPDATES TARGET_OFFER_MASTER RECORDS USING INPUT    *
      *    FILE RECEIVED THRU FTP UPLOADER, AFTER COMPLETE FEILD       *
      *    VALIDATION, ALL INVALID FEILDS ARE LOGGED IN A CONSOLIDATED *
      *    EXCEPTION REPORT.VALID ENTRIES ARE COMPARED WITH DB,        *
      *    UPDATED IF DIFFERENT AND COMMITED.                          *
      *    FINALLY, BACKUP IS GENERATED.                               *
      *                                                                *
      *    INPUT-FILE     : CCL*PRBUTMINP                              *
      *    EXCEPTION-FILE : CCL*PRBUTM01P                              *
      *                                                                *
      *  INPUT-FILE IN CSV FORMAT.                                     *
      *    OFFER_ID        - 6 DIGITS ONLY                             *
      *    MAX_REDEEM_CNT  - INTEGER (VALUES 1-99)                     *
      *    STATUS          - O (OPEN) / C (CLOSE)                      *
      *    BOOK_BEGIN_DATE - YYYYMMDD                                  *
      *    BOOK_END_DATE   - YYYYMMDD                                  *
      *    SAIL_BEGIN_DATE - YYYYMMDD                                  *
      *    SAIL_END_DATE   - YYYYMMDD                                  *
************************************************************************

       ENVIRONMENT DIVISION.
       CONFIGURATION SECTION.   
       SOURCE-COMPUTER. UNISYS. 
       OBJECT-COMPUTER. UNISYS  MEMORY SIZE 3 MODULES.  
       SPECIAL-NAMES.   
           PRINTER IS PRT   
           PRINTER IS PRINTER.  

       INPUT-OUTPUT SECTION.
       FILE-CONTROL.
           SELECT INPUT-FILE        ASSIGN TO DISC    PRBUTM01D.
           SELECT EXCEPTION-FILE    ASSIGN TO DISC    PRBUTM01P.

       DATA DIVISION.   
       SUBSCHEMA SECTION.   
       INVOKE SUBSCHEMA PST100UCOBSU
              IN FILE SUBSCHEMAS OF SCHEMA PST100SCHEMA 
              COPYING RECORDS    INTO COMMON
              COPYING DATA-NAMES INTO COMMON
              OVERLAY PAX-REC WITH PAX-CXF-REC  
                                   PAX-VI-REC   
              SAVE DATA INCLUDES DEFERRED-UPDATES   
              DMCA  COMMON  
              ERROR RECOVERY IS 9100-DATABASE-ERROR 
              ROLLBACK       IS 9100-DATABASE-ERROR.

           05  FILLER-E-3                       PIC X(01).  

       WORKING-STORAGE SECTION. 
       01  WS-PROG-ID                           PIC X(08) VALUE 
                                                            'PRB57UTM'. 
       01  MCFLAG                               PIC 9(10) BINARY.   

       01  WS-INPUT-REC.
           05 WS-INP-OFFER-ID                   PIC X(06).  
           05 WS-INP-MAX-REDEMPTION             PIC 9(03).

           05 WS-INVALID-NAME  OCCURS 20 TIMES  PIC X(40).

                                               PIC X(28).

           03 FILLER VALUE 'No of entries processed = ' 
                                               PIC X(28).   
           03 WS-HDG2-CNT                      PIC 9(10).   

       01  WS-HDG3-REC. 
           03 FILLER VALUE 'Total no. of exceptions = ' 
                                               PIC X(28).   
           03 WS-HDG3-CNT                      PIC 9(10).   

       01  WS-NUMERIC.  
           05  WS-INVALID-COUNT                 PIC 9(10) BINARY.   
           05  WS-EXCEPTION-COUNT               PIC 9(10) BINARY.   
           05  WS-MAX-REDEMPTION                PIC 9(10).  
           05  WS-INP-SEQ-NUM                   PIC 9(10) BINARY.   
           05  WS-SUB2                          PIC 9(10) BINARY.   
           05  WS-CNT                           PIC 9(10) BINARY.   
           05  WS-FREE-CTR                      PIC 9(10) BINARY.   
           05  WS-UPD-CNT                       PIC 9(10) BINARY.   
           05  WS-INP-UPD-CNT                   PIC 9(10) BINARY.   

       01  WS-ALPHANUMERIC. 
           05  WS-COMMENT                       PIC X(200) VALUE SPACES.
           05  WS-COMMENT-HOLD                  PIC X(200) VALUE SPACES.
           05  WS-I-USER-ID                     PIC X(16).  
           05  WS-DIFFER-FLAG                   PIC X(01).  
           05  WS-STATUS                        PIC X(01).  
           05  WS-START-BOOK-DATE               PIC X(08).  
           05  WS-END-BOOK-DATE                 PIC X(08).  
           05  WS-START-SAIL-DATE               PIC X(08).  
           05  WS-END-SAIL-DATE                 PIC X(08).  
           05  WS-SYSTEM-DATE                   PIC X(08).  
           05  WS-OFFER-ID-FINAL                PIC X(10).  
           05  WS-ACTIVITY-CODE                 PIC X(03).  
           05  WS-ACTIVITY-COMMENT              PIC X(225). 
           05  WS-MIN-OFFER-ID. 
               10  WS-MIN-OFFER-ID-FIRST-SIX    PIC X(06).  
               10  WS-MIN-OFFER-ID-LAST-FOUR    PIC X(04).  
           05  WS-MAX-OFFER-ID. 
               10  WS-MAX-OFFER-ID-FIRST-SIX    PIC X(06).  
               10  WS-MAX-OFFER-ID-LAST-FOUR    PIC X(04).  
           05  WS-RDMS-START-BOOK-DATE-TIME.
               10  WS-INP-START-BOOK-DATE-YYYY  PIC X(4).   
               10  FILLER                       PIC X  VALUE '-'.   
               10  WS-INP-START-BOOK-DATE-MM    PIC XX. 
               10  FILLER                       PIC X  VALUE '-'.   
               10  WS-INP-START-BOOK-DATE-DD    PIC XX. 
               10  WS-RDMS-START-DATE-TIME-BLK  PIC X.  
               10  WS-INP-START-BOOK-TIME-HH    PIC 9(02).  
               10  WS-RDMS-START-DATE-TIME-COL1 PIC X.  
               10  WS-INP-START-BOOK-TIME-MM    PIC 9(02).  
               10  WS-RDMS-START-DATE-TIME-COL2 PIC X.  
               10  WS-INP-START-BOOK-TIME-SS    PIC 9(02).  
           05  WS-RDMS-END-BOOK-DATE-TIME.  
               10  WS-INP-END-BOOK-DATE-YYYY    PIC X(4).   
               10  FILLER                       PIC X  VALUE '-'.   
               10  WS-INP-END-BOOK-DATE-MM      PIC XX. 
               10  FILLER                       PIC X  VALUE '-'.   
               10  WS-INP-END-BOOK-DATE-DD      PIC XX. 
               10  WS-RDMS-END-DATE-TIME-BLK    PIC X.  
               10  WS-INP-END-BOOK-TIME-HH      PIC 9(02).  
               10  WS-RDMS-END-DATE-TIME-COL1   PIC X.  
               10  WS-INP-END-BOOK-TIME-MM      PIC 9(02).  
               10  WS-RDMS-END-DATE-TIME-COL2   PIC X.  
               10  WS-INP-END-BOOK-TIME-SS      PIC 9(02).  
           05  WS-RDMS-INP-START-SAIL-DATE. 
               10  WS-INP-START-SAIL-DATE-YYYY  PIC X(4).   
               10  FILLER                       PIC X  VALUE '-'.   
               10  WS-INP-START-SAIL-DATE-MM    PIC XX. 
               10  FILLER                       PIC X  VALUE '-'.   
               10  WS-INP-START-SAIL-DATE-DD    PIC XX. 
           05  WS-RDMS-INP-END-SAIL-DATE.   
               10  WS-INP-END-SAIL-DATE-YYYY    PIC X(4).   
               10  FILLER                       PIC X  VALUE '-'.   
               10  WS-INP-END-SAIL-DATE-MM      PIC XX. 
               10  FILLER                       PIC X  VALUE '-'.   
               10  WS-INP-END-SAIL-DATE-DD      PIC XX. 

       COPY DPP01CIW.   
       COPY DPP01DTA.   
       COPY DPP01ERR.   
       COPY DPP01MCB.   
       COPY DPP01SCO.

       COPY SSP18GUD.   

       COMMON-STORAGE SECTION.  

       PROCEDURE DIVISION.  
       0000-MAIN-LINE.  
           COPY DPP01INT.   

           PERFORM DPP12-BEGIN-THREAD THRU  
                   DPP12-BEGIN-THREAD-EXIT. 

           IF  DPP01-SQL-ERROR-IND = 'Y'
               GO TO DPP12-THREAD-ERROR-EXIT.   

           EXEC SQL 
               USE DEFAULT SCHEMA PSTRDMS   
           END-EXEC.

           EXEC SQL WHENEVER SQLERROR   
                CONTINUE
           END-EXEC.

           EXEC SQL WHENEVER NOT FOUND  
                CONTINUE
           END-EXEC.

           OPEN INPUT  INPUT-FILE   
                OUTPUT EXCEPTION-FILE.  

           ACCEPT DPP02-YYYYMMDD-DATE FROM DATE YYYYMMDD.   
           MOVE DPP02-YYYYMMDD-DATE     TO WS-SYSTEM-DATE.  

           MOVE FUNCTION CURRENT-DATE   TO DPP01-SYSTEM-DATE-TIME.  

           IMPART.  

           OPEN DTRF USAGE-MODE IS UPDATE.  

           READ INPUT-FILE  
               AT END GO TO 9000-END-OF-JOB.

           UNSTRING INPUT-REC   
               DELIMITED BY ',' 
               INTO WS-I-USER-ID.   

           COPY SSP18INT.   

           IF WS-I-USER-ID = 'AUTOFTP-UTM'  
              MOVE 'SYS'                TO DPP01-3CHAR-USERID   
              MOVE 'AUTOFTP-UTM'        TO DPP01-USERID 
              CONTINUE  
           ELSE 
              MOVE FUNCTION UPPER-CASE(WS-I-USER-ID)
                                        TO SSP18-I-NT-UID   
              CALL 'SSC18GUD' USING        DPP01-COMMON-DATA-FIELDS 
                                           DPP01-COMMON-INFO
                                           SSP18-INPUT-PARAMS   
                                           SSP18-OUTPUT-PARAMS  

              IF DPP01-SUB-MSG-CTR > 0 AND  
                 DPP01-SUB-MSG-STATUS (1) > 1   
                 MOVE ZEROES            TO OUT-SEQ-NO   
                                           OUT-OFFER-ID 
                 MOVE 'User Id cannot be found' 
                                        TO OUT-EXCEPTION-COMMENT
                 ADD 1                  TO WS-EXCEPTION-COUNT   
                 WRITE EXCEPTION-REC
                 GO TO DPP12-END-OF-JOB 
              END-IF
              MOVE WS-I-USER-ID         TO DPP01-USERID 
              MOVE SSP18-O-NTUR-MAPPER-UID  
                                        TO DPP01-3CHAR-USERID   
           END-IF.  

      * To ignore header of input file. 
           READ INPUT-FILE  
               AT END GO TO 9000-END-OF-JOB.

           MOVE ZEROS                   TO WS-INP-UPD-CNT.  

       0100-READ-INPUT-PARAM.   

           MOVE ZEROS                   TO WS-UPD-CNT.  
           READ INPUT-FILE  
                AT END GO TO 9000-END-OF-JOB.   

           ADD  1                       TO WS-INP-SEQ-NUM.  

           MOVE ZEROS                   TO WS-CNT   
                                           OUT-SEQ-NO   
                                           WS-INVALID-COUNT.
           MOVE SPACES                  TO WS-COMMENT   
                                           OUT-OFFER-ID 
                                           OUT-EXCEPTION-COMMENT.   
           MOVE '|'                     TO FILLER-E-1   
                                           FILLER-E-2 FILLER-E-3.   

           UNSTRING INPUT-REC   
               DELIMITED BY ',' 
               INTO WS-INP-OFFER-ID 
                    WS-INP-MAX-REDEMPTION   
                    WS-INP-STATUS   
                    WS-INP-START-BOOK-DATE  
                    WS-INP-END-BOOK-DATE
                    WS-INP-START-SAIL-DATE  
                    WS-INP-END-SAIL-DATE.   

      *---------------------------VALIDATE EACH FEILD   
           IF  WS-INP-OFFER-ID <= SPACES
               ADD 1                    TO WS-INVALID-COUNT 
               MOVE 'OFFER-ID'          TO WS-INVALID-NAME  
                                        (WS-INVALID-COUNT)  
           END-IF.  

           IF  WS-INP-MAX-REDEMPTION < 1 OR WS-INP-MAX-REDEMPTION > 99  
               ADD 1                    TO WS-INVALID-COUNT 
               MOVE 'MAX-REDEMPTION'    TO WS-INVALID-NAME  
                                        (WS-INVALID-COUNT)  
           END-IF.  

           IF  (WS-INP-STATUS = 'O' OR 'C') 
               CONTINUE 
           ELSE 
               ADD 1                    TO WS-INVALID-COUNT 
               MOVE 'STATUS'            TO WS-INVALID-NAME  
                                        (WS-INVALID-COUNT)  
           END-IF.  

           IF  WS-INP-START-BOOK-DATE <= SPACES 
               ADD 1                    TO WS-INVALID-COUNT 
               MOVE 'BOOKING-BEGIN-DATE'
                                        TO WS-INVALID-NAME  
                                           (WS-INVALID-COUNT)   
           END-IF.  

           MOVE WS-INP-START-BOOK-DATE  TO DPP02-YYYYMMDD-DATE. 
           PERFORM DPP10-VALIDATE-YYYYMMDD-DATE.

           IF  DPP02-IS-DATE-VALID = 'N'
               ADD 1                    TO WS-INVALID-COUNT 
               MOVE 'BOOK-START-DATE'   TO WS-INVALID-NAME  
                                        (WS-INVALID-COUNT)  
           END-IF.  
           MOVE WS-INP-END-BOOK-DATE    TO DPP02-YYYYMMDD-DATE. 
           PERFORM DPP10-VALIDATE-YYYYMMDD-DATE.

           IF  DPP02-IS-DATE-VALID = 'N'
               ADD 1                    TO WS-INVALID-COUNT 
               MOVE 'BOOK-END-DATE'   TO WS-INVALID-NAME
                                        (WS-INVALID-COUNT)  
           END-IF.  

           MOVE WS-INP-START-SAIL-DATE  TO DPP02-YYYYMMDD-DATE. 
           PERFORM DPP10-VALIDATE-YYYYMMDD-DATE.

           IF  DPP02-IS-DATE-VALID = 'N'
               ADD 1                    TO WS-INVALID-COUNT 
               MOVE 'SAIL-START-DATE'   TO WS-INVALID-NAME  
                                        (WS-INVALID-COUNT)  
           END-IF.  

           MOVE WS-INP-END-SAIL-DATE    TO DPP02-YYYYMMDD-DATE. 
           PERFORM DPP10-VALIDATE-YYYYMMDD-DATE.

           IF  DPP02-IS-DATE-VALID = 'N'
               ADD 1                    TO WS-INVALID-COUNT 
               MOVE 'SAIL-END-DATE'   TO WS-INVALID-NAME
                                        (WS-INVALID-COUNT)  
           END-IF.  

           IF  WS-INP-END-BOOK-DATE <= WS-SYSTEM-DATE   
               ADD 1                    TO WS-INVALID-COUNT 
               MOVE 'BOOKING-END-DATE'  TO WS-INVALID-NAME  
                                        (WS-INVALID-COUNT)  
           END-IF.  

           IF  WS-INP-START-SAIL-DATE = SPACES  
               ADD 1                    TO WS-INVALID-COUNT 
               MOVE 'SAILING-BEGIN-DATE'
                                        TO WS-INVALID-NAME  
                                        (WS-INVALID-COUNT)  
           END-IF.  

           IF  WS-INP-END-SAIL-DATE <= WS-SYSTEM-DATE   
               ADD 1                    TO WS-INVALID-COUNT 
               MOVE 'SAILING-END-DATE'  TO WS-INVALID-NAME  
                                        (WS-INVALID-COUNT)  
           END-IF.  

           IF  WS-INP-END-BOOK-DATE <  WS-INP-START-BOOK-DATE   
               ADD 1                    TO WS-INVALID-COUNT 
               MOVE 'BOOK-END-DATE<BOOK-START-DATE' 
                                        TO WS-INVALID-NAME  
                                        (WS-INVALID-COUNT)  
           END-IF.  

           IF  WS-INP-END-SAIL-DATE < WS-INP-START-SAIL-DATE
               ADD 1                    TO WS-INVALID-COUNT 
               MOVE 'SAILING-END-DATE<SAILING-START-DATE'   
                                        TO WS-INVALID-NAME  
                                        (WS-INVALID-COUNT)  
           END-IF.  

           IF  WS-INP-END-SAIL-DATE < WS-INP-END-BOOK-DATE  
               ADD 1                    TO WS-INVALID-COUNT 
               MOVE 'SAILING-END-DATE<BOOK-END-DATE'
                                        TO WS-INVALID-NAME  
                                        (WS-INVALID-COUNT)  
           END-IF.  

           IF  WS-INVALID-COUNT > 0 
               PERFORM VARYING WS-SUB2 FROM 1 BY 1  
                   UNTIL WS-SUB2 > WS-INVALID-COUNT 
                   IF  WS-SUB2 = 1  
                       STRING WS-INVALID-NAME(WS-SUB2)  
                           DELIMITED BY SPACE INTO WS-COMMENT   
                   ELSE 
                       MOVE WS-COMMENT  TO WS-COMMENT-HOLD  
                       MOVE SPACES      TO WS-COMMENT   
                       STRING WS-COMMENT-HOLD   
                           DELIMITED BY SPACE ','   
                           WS-INVALID-NAME(WS-SUB2) 
                           DELIMITED BY SPACE INTO WS-COMMENT   
                   END-IF   
               END-PERFORM  
               STRING WS-COMMENT
                   DELIMITED BY SPACE ' INVALID.'   
                   DELIMITED BY SIZE INTO WS-COMMENT
               GO TO 0200-EXCEPTION 
           END-IF.  

           MOVE WS-INP-OFFER-ID         TO WS-MIN-OFFER-ID-FIRST-SIX
                                           WS-MAX-OFFER-ID-FIRST-SIX.   
           MOVE '0000'                  TO WS-MIN-OFFER-ID-LAST-FOUR.   
           MOVE '9999'                  TO WS-MAX-OFFER-ID-LAST-FOUR.   

       0100-PROCESS-UPDATE. 

           EXEC SQL 
                DECLARE OFFER_CURSOR CURSOR FOR RETENTION   
                    SELECT OFFER_ID,
                           IFNULL(MAX_REDEEM_CNT,99),   
                           STATUS_CODE, 
                           CONVERT(BOOK_BEGIN_DATETIME,112),
                           CONVERT(BOOK_END_DATETIME,112),  
                           CONVERT(SAIL_BEGIN_DATE,112),
                           CONVERT(SAIL_END_DATE,112)   
                    FROM TARGET_OFFER_MASTER
                    WHERE (OFFER_ID BETWEEN :WS-MIN-OFFER-ID AND
                                         :WS-MAX-OFFER-ID ) 
           END-EXEC.

           EXEC SQL 
               OPEN OFFER_CURSOR
           END-EXEC.

       0200-FETCH-NEXT-CURSOR.  

           MOVE ZEROS                   TO WS-MAX-REDEMPTION.   
           MOVE SPACES                  TO WS-OFFER-ID-FINAL
                                           WS-STATUS
                                           WS-START-BOOK-DATE   
                                           WS-END-BOOK-DATE 
                                           WS-START-SAIL-DATE   
                                           WS-END-SAIL-DATE.

           EXEC SQL 
              FETCH NEXT FROM OFFER_CURSOR  
              INTO :WS-OFFER-ID-FINAL,  
                   :WS-MAX-REDEMPTION,  
                   :WS-STATUS,  
                   :WS-START-BOOK-DATE, 
                   :WS-END-BOOK-DATE,   
                   :WS-START-SAIL-DATE, 
                   :WS-END-SAIL-DATE
           END-EXEC.

           IF  SQLCODE < 0  
               DISPLAY 'ERROR IN FETCH NEXT' UPON PRINTER   
               GO TO 9150-RDMS-ERROR
           END-IF.  

           IF  SQLCODE = 100
               IF  WS-CNT  = 0  
                   DISPLAY 'ERROR IN 1ST FETCH' UPON PRINTER
                   MOVE WS-INP-OFFER-ID TO WS-OFFER-ID-FINAL
                   MOVE 'ERROR IN FETCH'
                                        TO WS-COMMENT   
                   EXEC SQL 
                       CLOSE OFFER_CURSOR   
                   END-EXEC 
                   GO TO 0200-EXCEPTION 
                ELSE
                   DISPLAY 'TOTAL COUNT OF UPDATED OFFER_ID : ' 
                       WS-INP-OFFER-ID ' = ' WS-UPD-CNT UPON PRINTER
                   EXEC SQL 
                       CLOSE OFFER_CURSOR   
                   END-EXEC 
                   GO TO 0100-READ-INPUT-PARAM  
                END-IF  
           END-IF.  

           ADD  1                       TO WS-CNT.  

           MOVE 'N'                     TO WS-DIFFER-FLAG.  

           IF  WS-MAX-REDEMPTION NOT = WS-INP-MAX-REDEMPTION
               MOVE SPACES              TO WS-ACTIVITY-COMMENT  
               MOVE 'Y'                 TO WS-DIFFER-FLAG   
               MOVE 'TOM'               TO WS-ACTIVITY-CODE 
               STRING 'MAX REDEEM CNT CHG FROM: '   
                       WS-MAX-REDEMPTION DELIMITED BY SIZE  
                      ' TO: '   
                      WS-INP-MAX-REDEMPTION  DELIMITED BY SIZE  
                 INTO WS-ACTIVITY-COMMENT   
               PERFORM 2400-STORE-TOM-HISTORY THRU  
                       2400-EXIT
           END-IF.  

           IF  WS-STATUS NOT = WS-INP-STATUS
               MOVE 'Y'                 TO WS-DIFFER-FLAG   
               MOVE 'TOC'               TO WS-ACTIVITY-CODE 
               MOVE SPACES              TO WS-ACTIVITY-COMMENT  
               IF  WS-INP-STATUS = 'C'  
                   MOVE 'OFFER DEACTIVATED' 
                                        TO WS-ACTIVITY-COMMENT  
               ELSE 
                   MOVE 'OFFER ACTIVATED'   
                                        TO WS-ACTIVITY-COMMENT  
               END-IF   
               PERFORM 2400-STORE-TOM-HISTORY THRU  
                       2400-EXIT
           END-IF.  

           IF  WS-START-BOOK-DATE NOT = WS-INP-START-BOOK-DATE  
               MOVE 'Y'                 TO WS-DIFFER-FLAG   
               MOVE 'TOB'               TO WS-ACTIVITY-CODE 
               MOVE SPACES              TO WS-ACTIVITY-COMMENT  
               STRING 'BOOK EFF-DATE CHG FROM: ' WS-START-BOOK-DATE 
                      ' TO: ' WS-INP-START-BOOK-DATE DELIMITED BY SIZE  
                  INTO WS-ACTIVITY-COMMENT  
               PERFORM 2400-STORE-TOM-HISTORY THRU  
                       2400-EXIT
           END-IF.  

           IF  WS-END-BOOK-DATE NOT = WS-INP-END-BOOK-DATE  
               MOVE 'Y'                 TO WS-DIFFER-FLAG   
               MOVE 'TOB'               TO WS-ACTIVITY-CODE 
               MOVE SPACES              TO WS-ACTIVITY-COMMENT  
               STRING 'BOOK END-DATE CHG FROM: ' WS-END-BOOK-DATE   
                      ' TO: ' WS-INP-END-BOOK-DATE DELIMITED BY SIZE
                  INTO WS-ACTIVITY-COMMENT  
               PERFORM 2400-STORE-TOM-HISTORY THRU  
                       2400-EXIT
           END-IF.  

           IF  WS-START-SAIL-DATE NOT = WS-INP-START-SAIL-DATE  
               MOVE 'Y'                 TO WS-DIFFER-FLAG   
               MOVE 'TOB'               TO WS-ACTIVITY-CODE 
               MOVE SPACES              TO WS-ACTIVITY-COMMENT  
               STRING 'SAIL EFF-DATE CHG FROM: ' WS-START-SAIL-DATE 
                      ' TO: ' WS-INP-START-SAIL-DATE DELIMITED BY SIZE  
                  INTO WS-ACTIVITY-COMMENT  
               PERFORM 2400-STORE-TOM-HISTORY THRU  
                       2400-EXIT
           END-IF.  

           IF  WS-END-SAIL-DATE NOT = WS-INP-END-SAIL-DATE  
               MOVE 'Y'                 TO WS-DIFFER-FLAG   
               MOVE 'TOD'               TO WS-ACTIVITY-CODE 
               MOVE SPACES              TO WS-ACTIVITY-COMMENT  
               STRING 'SAIL END-DATE CHG FROM: ' WS-END-SAIL-DATE   
                      ' TO: ' WS-INP-END-SAIL-DATE DELIMITED BY SIZE
                  INTO WS-ACTIVITY-COMMENT  
               PERFORM 2400-STORE-TOM-HISTORY THRU  
                       2400-EXIT
           END-IF.  

           IF  WS-DIFFER-FLAG = 'N' 
               DISPLAY 'NO DIFFERENCE FOUND FOR OFFER : '   
                                     WS-INP-OFFER-ID UPON PRINTER   
               MOVE 'NO DIFFERENCE FOUND FOR OFFER '
                                        TO WS-COMMENT   
               MOVE WS-INP-SEQ-NUM      TO OUT-SEQ-NO   
               MOVE WS-OFFER-ID-FINAL   TO OUT-OFFER-ID 
               MOVE WS-COMMENT          TO OUT-EXCEPTION-COMMENT
               ADD 1                    TO WS-EXCEPTION-COUNT   
               WRITE EXCEPTION-REC  
               GO TO 0200-FETCH-NEXT-CURSOR 
           END-IF.  

      *----------------UPDATE DB IF DIFF FOUND  

           MOVE WS-INP-START-BOOK-DATE  TO DPP02-DMS-DATE.  
           PERFORM DPP10-DMS-TO-RDMS-DATETIME.  
           MOVE DPP02-RDMS-DATE         TO WS-RDMS-START-BOOK-DATE-TIME.

           MOVE WS-INP-END-BOOK-DATE    TO DPP02-DMS-DATE.  
           PERFORM DPP10-DMS-TO-RDMS-DATETIME.  
           MOVE DPP02-RDMS-DATE         TO WS-RDMS-END-BOOK-DATE-TIME.  

           MOVE ' '                     TO WS-RDMS-START-DATE-TIME-BLK  
                                           WS-RDMS-END-DATE-TIME-BLK.   

           MOVE ':'                     TO WS-RDMS-START-DATE-TIME-COL1 
                                           WS-RDMS-START-DATE-TIME-COL2 
                                           WS-RDMS-END-DATE-TIME-COL1   
                                           WS-RDMS-END-DATE-TIME-COL2.  

           MOVE ZEROES                  TO WS-INP-START-BOOK-TIME-HH
                                           WS-INP-START-BOOK-TIME-MM
                                           WS-INP-START-BOOK-TIME-SS.   

           MOVE 23                      TO WS-INP-END-BOOK-TIME-HH  
           MOVE 59                      TO WS-INP-END-BOOK-TIME-MM  
           MOVE 59                      TO WS-INP-END-BOOK-TIME-SS. 

           MOVE WS-INP-START-SAIL-DATE  TO DPP02-DMS-DATE.  
           PERFORM DPP10-DMS-TO-RDMS-DATETIME.  
           MOVE DPP02-RDMS-DATE         TO WS-RDMS-INP-START-SAIL-DATE. 

           MOVE WS-INP-END-SAIL-DATE    TO DPP02-DMS-DATE.  
           PERFORM DPP10-DMS-TO-RDMS-DATETIME.  
           MOVE DPP02-RDMS-DATE         TO WS-RDMS-INP-END-SAIL-DATE.   

           EXEC SQL 
                UPDATE TARGET_OFFER_MASTER  
                SET MAX_REDEEM_CNT      = :WS-INP-MAX-REDEMPTION,   
                    STATUS_CODE         = :WS-INP-STATUS,   
                    BOOK_BEGIN_DATETIME = :WS-RDMS-START-BOOK-DATE-TIME,
                    BOOK_END_DATETIME   = :WS-RDMS-END-BOOK-DATE-TIME,  
                    SAIL_BEGIN_DATE     = :WS-RDMS-INP-START-SAIL-DATE, 
                    SAIL_END_DATE       = :WS-RDMS-INP-END-SAIL-DATE,   
                    UPDATE_DATETIME     =  GETDATE()
                WHERE OFFER_ID          = :WS-OFFER-ID-FINAL
           END-EXEC.

           ADD 1                        TO WS-UPD-CNT   
                                           WS-FREE-CTR. 
           IF  SQLCODE < 0  
               GO TO 9150-RDMS-ERROR
           END-IF.  

           IF WS-UPD-CNT = 1
              ADD 1                     TO WS-INP-UPD-CNT   
           END-IF.  

           IF  WS-FREE-CTR > 0  
               PERFORM DPP12-COMMIT THRU DPP12-COMMIT-EXIT  
               MOVE 0                   TO WS-FREE-CTR  
           END-IF.  

           MOVE 'OFFER ID UPDATED SUCCESSFULLY '
                                        TO WS-COMMENT.  
           MOVE WS-INP-SEQ-NUM          TO OUT-SEQ-NO.  
           MOVE WS-OFFER-ID-FINAL       TO OUT-OFFER-ID.
           MOVE WS-COMMENT              TO OUT-EXCEPTION-COMMENT.   
           WRITE EXCEPTION-REC. 

           GO TO 0200-FETCH-NEXT-CURSOR.

       0200-EXCEPTION.  
           MOVE WS-INP-SEQ-NUM          TO OUT-SEQ-NO.  
           MOVE WS-INP-OFFER-ID         TO OUT-OFFER-ID.
           MOVE WS-COMMENT              TO OUT-EXCEPTION-COMMENT.   
           ADD 1                        TO WS-EXCEPTION-COUNT.  
           WRITE EXCEPTION-REC. 
           GO TO 0100-READ-INPUT-PARAM. 

       2400-STORE-TOM-HISTORY.

                      (OFFER_ID

                      ,ACTIVITY_CODE
                      ,USER_ID  
                      ,COMMENT) 
              VALUES (:WS-OFFER-ID-FINAL,   
                       GETUTCDATE(),
                      :WS-ACTIVITY-CODE,
                      :DPP01-USERID,
                      :WS-ACTIVITY-COMMENT) 
           END-EXEC.

           IF  SQLCODE < 0  
               DISPLAY 'ERROR IN INSERT' UPON PRINTER   
               GO TO 9150-RDMS-ERROR
           END-IF.  

           PERFORM 2500-STORE-DTRF THRU 
                   2500-STORE-DTRF-EXIT.

       2400-EXIT.

       2500-STORE-DTRF. 
      *   Store DTRF Record to Track TGO Disabling *
           MOVE SPACES                  TO DTRF-REC.
           MOVE 'R'                     TO DTRF-CALC-KEY (1:1). 
           MOVE WS-OFFER-ID-FINAL       TO DTRF-CALC-KEY (2:10).
           MOVE DPP01-SYSTEM-DATE       TO DTRF-ACTIVITY-DATE.  
           DIVIDE DPP01-SYSTEM-TIME BY 100 GIVING DTRF-ACTIVITY-TIME.   
           MOVE WS-ACTIVITY-CODE        TO DTRF-ACTIVITY-CODE.  
           MOVE 'PRB57UTM'              TO DTRF-OPER-INITIALS.  
           MOVE ZEROS                   TO DTRF-PHONE-EXT   
                                           DTRF-PAX-SEQ-NUM (1) 
                                           DTRF-PAX-SEQ-NUM (2) 
                                           DTRF-PAX-SEQ-NUM (3) 
                                           DTRF-PAX-SEQ-NUM (4) 
                                           DTRF-PAX-SEQ-NUM (5).

           STORE DTRF-REC.  

           DISPLAY 'STORED DTRF FOR ' DTRF-CALC-KEY UPON PRINTER.

           EXIT.
