      ****************************************************************  
      *       Load a COMPANY record into Internal Cache              *  
      ****************************************************************  
      * Description:                                                 *  
      *     The purpose of this FLSS routine is to load a central    *  
      *     cache with a specific company (CMP), company reference   *  
      *     (CRR), and/or company threshold (CTR) data record.       *  
      *                                                              *  
      *     It will reside in a Sub-system that is built and         *  
      *     installed.  Changes to this require the sub-system to    *  
      *     be rebuilt.  As long as the interface is not altered     *  
      *     calling programs don't need to be re-compiled.           *  
      *                                                              *  
      * Input      DPP01-COMMON-DATA-FIELDS                          *  
      *            DPP01-COMMON-INFO                                 *  
      *            SSP15-INPUT-PARAMS                                *  
      *                                                              *  
      * Output     SSP15-OUTPUT-PARMS                                *  
      *                                                              *  
      * Calling Sequence                                             *  
      *     CALL 'SSO15-LOAD-CMP'   USING DPP01-COMMON-DATA-FIELDS   *  
      *                                   DPP01-COMMON-INFO          *  
      *                                   SSP15-INPUT-PARAMS         *  
      *                                   SSP15-OUTPUT-PARAMS.       *  
      *                                                              *  
      *     CALL 'SSO15-LOAD-CCR'   USING DPP01-COMMON-DATA-FIELDS   *  
      *                                   DPP01-COMMON-INFO          *  
      *                                   SSP15-INPUT-PARAMS         *  
      *                                   SSP15-OUTPUT-PARAMS.       *  
      *                                                              *  
      *     CALL 'SSO15-LOAD-CTR'   USING DPP01-COMMON-DATA-FIELDS   *  
      *                                   DPP01-COMMON-INFO          *  
      *                                   SSP15-INPUT-PARAMS         *  
      *                                   SSP15-OUTPUT-PARAMS.       *  
      *                                                              *  
      * Return Status Values:                                        *  
      *                00       OK                                   *  
      *                01       Informational                        *  
      *                02       RPC Error                            *  
      *                03       Fatal or Severe Error                *  
      ****************************************************************
